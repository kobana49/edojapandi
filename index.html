What we make

Edo Japandi takes celebrated woodblock prints by masters such as Hokusai, Hiroshige, Kuniyoshi and Utamaro — images that have entered the public domain — and carefully prepares them as high-resolution, ready-to-print wall art. Every piece is sourced from open museum collections, color-corrected, and restored with respect for the original block work.

The result sits quietly in a contemporary room: the spirit of Edo, the restraint of Scandinavian-Japanese design.

How we work
