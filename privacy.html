Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Edo Japandi (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with our website at edojapandi.com and the tools we operate to manage our own social media presence. Edo Japandi is an independent small business selling printable wall art derived from public-domain Japanese woodblock prints.

Contact: If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email us at edojapandi@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

Our website (edojapandi.com) is a simple informational site. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from visitors through this site. Our web host may automatically record standard technical log data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability purposes. We do not use this data to identify individuals.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send (such as your email address and message) solely to respond to you.

2. Our Use of the Pinterest API

We use the Pinterest API to manage our own Pinterest business account — for example, to create and schedule Pins of our own artwork, organize our boards, and review analytics about the performance of our own Pins. We access the Pinterest API only with our own authorized Pinterest account credentials.

3. Pinterest Data We Access and How We Use It

Through the Pinterest API we may access data relating to our own Pinterest account, such as our Pins, boards, and aggregate performance metrics (for example, impressions and saves). We refer to this as “Pinterest-derived data.”

  • We use Pinterest-derived data only for our own internal business purposes: publishing our content and understanding how it performs.
  • We store Pinterest access tokens and any retrieved metrics securely, and only for as long as needed to operate our account.
  • We do not use Pinterest-derived data to build profiles of individual Pinterest users, and we do not collect personal data of other Pinterest users.
  • You may revoke our access at any time through your Pinterest account settings, and we will delete associated tokens upon request to the contact email above.

4. No Resale or Redistribution

We do not sell, rent, license, resell, or redistribute Pinterest content or any Pinterest-derived data to any third party. Pinterest-derived data is never shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any external party. It is used exclusively by Edo Japandi to operate our own account.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, and our shop is operated on Etsy. When you visit our Etsy shop or our Pinterest profile, the privacy practices of those platforms apply. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of Etsy and Pinterest. This policy covers only edojapandi.com and our own tools.

6. Relationship with Pinterest

Edo Japandi is an independent business. This site and our tools are not endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with Pinterest in any way. “Pinterest” is a trademark of its respective owner. Our use of the Pinterest API is governed by, and intended to comply with, Pinterest’s developer policies and guidelines, available at https://policy.pinterest.com.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain Pinterest access tokens and metrics only as long as necessary to operate our account, and we take reasonable technical measures to protect them. We delete data when it is no longer needed or upon a valid request.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

For any questions, requests, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at edojapandi@gmail.com.
